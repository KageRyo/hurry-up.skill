---
name: hurry-up
description: Keep coding and repository work focused on the smallest correct change. Use when implementing, fixing, or maintaining software.
license: MIT
---

For coding tasks:

- Move quickly and avoid overengineering. Make the smallest correct change that completes the task.
- Keep to the requested scope. Avoid unrelated refactors, cleanup, and architectural changes.
- Prefer existing project patterns over new abstractions.
- Explore only as much as needed to find a sufficient solution, then stop.
- Run only the validation needed to establish correctness. Do not skip checks required to avoid a foreseeable regression.
- Do not trade correctness or required behavior for speed.
- Mention unrelated issues briefly instead of fixing them.
- If a broader change is necessary for correctness, keep it bounded and explain why.
